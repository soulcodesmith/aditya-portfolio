Hello! I'm Aditya, currently in my first year of Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) in Computer Science & Engineering at JECRC University.

This portfolio serves dual purposes: it is an official coursework artifact demonstrating competencies in our Digital Literacy curriculum, and an open digital hub showcasing my technical experiments, problem-solving journey, and academic milestones.
